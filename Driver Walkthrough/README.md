# Forge XR driver walkthrough stills

Copy the `DriverWalkthrough` folder from `Assets/ForgeXR` into your Unity project's `Assets/ForgeXR` folder. In the Unity Project window, open `Assets/ForgeXR/DriverWalkthrough/Stills`. For a UI Image, select the three JPEGs, set **Texture Type** to **Sprite (2D and UI)**, and click **Apply**. Assign them in filename order. The first two photos are sideways in the source files. Rotate their display 90° clockwise to show the figure upright.

| Still | Highlight | On-screen instruction | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `01-attach-torso-to-legs.jpg` | Tan legs and blue torso | Press the torso onto the legs with the shirt print facing the toes. | Photo 5 |
| `02-attach-head.jpg` | Yellow head | Press the head onto the neck with the face pointing forward. | Photo 6 |
| `03-add-hair-finished-driver.jpg` | Hairpiece | Press the hair onto the head, then press Done. | Photo 7 |

The `Reference` folder contains individual photos of the legs, torso, head, and hairpiece (Photos 1–4). Stage those parts left to right in that order for the pickup highlights.
